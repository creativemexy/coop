# Brag Plan: FENAC COOP — The 60s cinematic cut

## What is this app?

FENAC COOP is a member-owned digital cooperative bank in Nigeria: savings with compounding interest, BNPL buying power, member-rate loans, cooperative investments, and native iOS + Android apps — one warm financial home.

## The angle

Treat FENAC COOP like a blockbuster: this is a $500k-budget launch film for a bank that belongs to its members. Trailer-scale motion, deep cocoa and gold light, big editorial type, a real logo, and real app features doing real things — savings goals advancing, purchases splitting into installments, rates counting down, returns compounding.

## Hook (first 6 seconds)

Cold open on deep cocoa. Grain and a slow golden light sweep pull the FENAC COOP logo into being. The line **"Building wealth together."** locks under it on a strong beat.

## Key moments (the middle)

- **The collective, in scale**: 10,000+ members, ₦2.4B savings pool, 35+ cooperatives — giant counters ticking up on strong cues (users flow: scale matters more than any pitch).
- **Join in minutes**: a phone shows tap → KYC verifying (progress ring) → Unlocked. The real membership flow.
- **One warm dashboard**: the ivory member dashboard, Savings Pool hero card first, then Savings, BNPL, Loans, Investments cards snapping to the beat — the pool number is from the live site.
- **The app arrives**: iOS and Android handsets lift in with the warm mobile app on both.
- **App — Savings & BNPL**: a savings goal grows with a deposit; a purchase splits into installments you can actually read.
- **App — Loans & Investments**: a loan amount settles onto a low honest rate; investments tick up a compounded return.
- **Built for the member**: four proof pillars settle two-by-two, then the member stats strip locks in.

## Outro / punchline

The logo returns at full scale, **FENAC COOP** slams in, **Building Wealth Together.** lands, and the final bell rings over a music swell.
**www.fenacoop.org**

## User flow worth showing

Join the cooperative → KYC verification → one dashboard for saving, buying, borrowing, and investing → the same experience in the native iOS + Android app.
