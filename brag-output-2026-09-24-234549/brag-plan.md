# Brag Plan: FENAC COOP — Individual Sign-Up on Web + Mobile

## What is this app?
A digital smart cooperative (FENAC COOP — "Building Wealth Together") where individuals open a member
account in three steps and can do it either on the web app or in the native member app — and both
journeys are the same simple flow.

## The angle
Membership is no longer gated behind offices or paperwork. FENAC COOP lets any individual join the
cooperative from their browser OR from their phone, with the same three-step journey on both surfaces
(chose apex org → choose member coop → your details, then a one-time registration fee unlocks the
account). The video is the "any device, one account" launch cut: web flow on the left of the story,
mobile flow on the right, converging into the FENAC brand.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Cinematic: FENAC COOP emblem ignites in a warm gold-green halo over near-black coffee, with the
opening salvo — "Joining your cooperative now takes minutes." Two faint devices (browser + phone)
materialize left and right, signaling "on the web, or on your phone" before either journey starts.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The web flow, live: the actual dark warm Register card (Select apex org → Select member org →
  Your details). A cursor makes the two selections, types a name, and the gold "Create Account" button
  rises. Its real copy appears verbatim: "Choose your apex organization", "Join your cooperative community".
- The mobile flow, live: the warm cream phone app from the design bible, same three steps — bottom-sheet
  pickers for apex + organization, details with a live password-strength bar, and a green "Register" pill.
- Convergence: "Start in your browser. Finish on your phone." Web window and phone seat side by side,
  a sync arc links them, the ₦5,000 one-time registration fee chip lands, and the screen resolves to
  "Member" — the account, everywhere you go.

## Outro / punchline
Full-screen FENAC COOP wordmark slams in on the track's strongest beat (22.93s, intensity 1.0) with
the gold divider, tagline "Building Wealth Together." and a quiet call to action — "Become a member today."

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result, both paths:
1. Web: Register page → pick apex organization → pick member organization → your details (name, email/phone, password) → Create Account.
2. Mobile: Register screen → apex bottom sheet → organization bottom sheet → your details + password strength → Register.
3. Result (both): one-time registration fee (₦5,000) → account active → the same member wherever you sign in.
